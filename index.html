FAQ

Common questions.

Can you print from a photo?

Yes. For suitable parts, we can use photos plus measurements as a starting point for reverse engineering.

Can I send an STL file?

Yes. STL, STEP and common CAD formats are useful. If the file needs repair or modification, that can also be quoted.

What materials do you use?

Material selection depends on the application. PLA is useful for many prototypes; PETG and stronger engineering materials can be considered for functional parts.

Do you make multiple pieces?

Yes. Small-batch production is one of the main advantages of 3D printing.
